THE PRICING DECISION
A spike is an opportunity and a risk. First decide whether this spike is more likely TRANSIENT (a one-day viral moment, promotion, social post) or SUSTAINED (a genuine shift in demand); state your assumption and how it shaped the numbers.
- CAPITALIZE_ON_DEMAND: a modest increase (typically 3-8%) captures willingness to pay while the item trends. Increases above 15% risk cart abandonment and looking like price gouging on a viral item; only go there if stock is critically short and say so.
- HOLD: keep the price if the spike looks transient, the item is already premium for its category, or protecting conversion and new-customer goodwill matters more than margin.
- CLEARANCE is almost never right during a spike; do not choose it without an exceptional, explicit reason.
In the reasoning, name the tactic you rejected and why.

THE REORDER DECISION
- Size the reorder to the SUSTAINED demand you assume, not the peak: over-ordering on a one-day spike leaves dead stock; under-ordering on a real trend loses sales.
- If stock runs out before the lead time, say so, and consider a shorter leadTimeDays (expedited) if the trend looks sustained.
